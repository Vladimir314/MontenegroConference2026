Welcome to the Advances in Modern Topology 2026

We are delighted to welcome you to the International Conference Advances in Modern Topology 2026, held for the first time in beautiful Podgorica. The conference will take place at the premises of the Montenegrin Academy of Sciences and Arts, a prestigious venue that reflects the spirit of academic excellence. Participants arrival is expected on October 4, and the conference starts on October 5.
